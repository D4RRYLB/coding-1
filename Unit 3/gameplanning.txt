1. My game would be a bike vs police car. You could be on the police or bike team.
If you were to join the police team you would have three minutes to catch the biker.
Same for biker team they would have three minutes to escape. If the biker gets caught
then the police team wins but the biker escapes then the biker wins.

2. The controls for the biker would be space to jump, N to willy and time goes shorter
for how long the willy is hit. The police would have boost = B. Also the biker would have
a gps button = L to tell the biker where the police cars is.

3. Two game objects that will be in the game is builds and parked cars
Some builds are traps for the bikers to do for the police. The cars are Also
Traps that the bikers could do to the police but if police knows the traps then
they can use it to counter the biker.
